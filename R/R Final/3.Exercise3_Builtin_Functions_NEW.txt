7.apply function

apply(), which operates on arrays. 
For simplicity, the tutorial limits itself to 2D arrays, which are also known as matrices.


apply(X, MARGIN, FUN, ...)

MARGIN=1, it applies over rows, 
MARGIN=2, it works over columns. 
MARGIN=c(1,2), it applies to both rows and columns


x=matrix(1:100,10,10)
apply(x,1,sum)
apply(x,2,sum)
