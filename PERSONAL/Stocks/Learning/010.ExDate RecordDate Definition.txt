Ex Dividend Date:
The date on or after which if you buy the shares with the aim of getting dividend, you will not get it.(due to T+2 delivery cycle)

so you have to buy the Shares before the Ex Dividend Date for getting Dividend.


Book Closure Date / Record Date :
The date on which Shares should be available in your demat account in order to receive the dividend.
even on the next day if you sell the shares, absolutely no problem, as your shares are already accounted for Dividend.

Generally, Investors will sell the shares after the record date as they knew their shares are already accounted. 
hence share price will decrease on the date immediately after the Record Date for sure.
